My schedule was all go this week. Until now, I had the chance to throw a birthday party for him. Even when I intended to do so on the exact day, his wife manifestly took precedence over me. However, I was glad that he still remembered me, remembered days of mutually celebrating birthdays.

To be honest, I was weary of appearing on camera and speaking. They even went to the Puerto or my nest to bother not only me but the people around. At such times, he was the one kindly asking them to leave. I did not know whether they cared about my feelings or simply piggybacked over my claims to boost their brands’ images. I, as a matter of fact, did have plans to disappear. A few years from now, I would be withdrawing from the media.

We still met at that familiar beach. I had bought a birthday cake with the words “Pedro the Padre”. He indeed was going to have his own angel. I did not know if the baby was a boy or a girl, but I could feel the excitement in his tone. He had always ached for a perfect family, and now he could have a good laugh. I was very happy for him. Thus, I was conferred the title of “God father”. On that day, he hoped that I would teach his child literature, so that he or she could be as top-notch as me. I just smiled and told him that he did a good job already.

Whilst I was having lunch break in the cabin, he came to me with the recognisable routines, smoking and talking nonsense. He said that he was the happiest man on earth, as he had a beautiful wife, a child to come, and a best fella, me myself. I smiled and said that he was cunning and raised a toast to congratulate him on his jollity. But my eyes were so brazen. Rather than showing joy, they gleamed with envy beyond a shadow of doubt. It was just that he trusted and spoiled me, so he could barely recognise the pettiness in me. What a shame!

After work, he wanted to take me home, but I said: “I still want to watch the sunset for a little longer”. In truth, it was but an excuse. I already knew that had he not had his own family, I would have been his priority. But life changed. My standing with him shifted, and I, as a result, clearly had no reason to bar his way. The motorbike’s roar dissipated into the atmosphere. He left, but what he left was not only me, but a feeling of incredible emptiness.

I took out the pencil and the notebook from my canvas tote bag and embarked on sketching the imposing landscape before me. Although I saw it almost every day, sitting down and reflecting on it and on myself was sort of a leisure pursuit. Back then, when he was still here, I felt like I was accompanied; yet, he had his own love now. It seemed to me that I had better find some diversion for myself to fill his absence, which helped hone myself for the middle age later.

I stroked my hair. It was long enough to donate. But I occasionally asked myself if I truly wished to cut this hair. Doing good is exemplary, but what if the good goes against our will? He once told me to let it be, and though I, of course, would be the one making the final decision, he remained on my side eventually. “What is ours is ours. What is not ours is never ours, regardless of how poor we are or how hard we try to plead for. It will slip through our fingers,” I told myself.

Immediately after the disappearance of the dusk, I had everything prepared. Recently, I was wondering about finding someone to fill his absence, as if I were trying to prove that without him, I still had other choices. So far, homosexuality had been accepted more than before, but telling him the truth was what made me undecided. I meant that hiding truths was bad, but if the truths possibly broke down the relationship between us, I believed it would be a wise move to maintain silence. Since he said that he did not care about homosexual things, why should I utter then? Being with him in this heterosexual condition was better than telling the truth, which might bewilder him and compel him to accept my new me.

The search notwithstanding, I could hardly find anyone to fill the emptiness he left in me. There was no one but him able to do so. There was no one but him able to enter my life. And there was no one but him able to open my dust-covered gate. But everything had its own fate. Mine was solitude, a comet that kept flying in the universe and never had a fixed destination. “I wonder if he leaves everything behind and runs with me.”

“What are you thinking, Ant?”

Startled, I said: “Nothing. Just keep going.”

“What’s wrong? Are you thinking of someone?”

“No.” I smiled. “I said I’d be a virgin for rest of my life.”

“Bullshit. You’ll find your ideal woman in the end.”

“Will I, daddy?”

He shyly nodded. As a to-be father, he appeared to strive to be a model figure for his offspring. In reality, he had always strived to achieve what he wanted. At the present, he was content with what he had. He, nevertheless, once told me that life was unpredictable. He was afraid that living for the present and losing sight of the difficulties ahead might summon some serious repercussions. He was testament to the differential between being academically properly educated and being experientially vitally educated. In my writing works, he was always the protagonist, a person influential in spreading positivity to the people around. And there, I was always the antagonist, who loved making trouble and ruining everything he built.

“I have an idea.”

“What?”

“Why don’t we go swimming?”

“You’re energetic. Besides, you’ve just bounced back, so please don’t do such a stupid thing.”

“I’m fit as a flea. I suddenly remember our days of swimming here, so I’m sort of in high spirits. You go?”

“Of course… Yes!” I shouted.

He held my hand rushing to the sea like fish saw water. We played all manners of things, like splashing, diving, and wrestling. The saltiness, however, caused me to cry. I quickly immersed my tears into the current so as not to preoccupy him. I was supposed to be happy. Truth to be told, I had no idea why I cried. I had money, career, and a good fella. Why did I cry so desperately like this? I tried to scream into the water, as though I were releasing my ever-present anger. Even when I shouted, the water was immune to the negative feelings of me.

A while later, we went back to my home. After getting everything in place, I told him to make a wish as tradition dictated. He looked at me and said: “Again, thank you so much.”

“Idiot, why are you becoming a stranger?”

“Tonight, we won’t leave till we’re drunk.”

“I hope you’re drunk.”

“Why?” he frowned.”

“Then you’ll leave and won’t take my bed.”

He chuckled. “So if I’m drunk, please lend me your ancient bed.”

“Oh, do I sleep on the floor?”

“I’m the spotlight.”

“You’re so authoritative!”

Then we lit some fags and drank some beers. He became serious and began to talk about life more. He also asked me about my red eyes, but I lied and said that it was because of the salty water. He apologised for dragging me in his fun, but I told him that I was very happy. There lay a fact that he was much happier than before. Even though we did not stand by each other often, he did give me something special to me, a thing that his wife did not have. This made me slightly proud.

“Ant. I’ve read some of your books. Some are fairly strange to me, but my wife and I love the others.”

“You’re my VIP, so I make an exception for you to read my publications first.”

“I know that you’re hiding me something…”

“I am not.”

“You’re still stubborn.”

“It’s my character.”

“That’s why you’re still a virgin.”

I stared at him and said: “Are you making fun of me?”

He said nothing but came closer and brushed my hair aside. “You’re dressing too lightly.” Then he stroked my shoulder and took a deep sniff in those strands of hair, whispering: “You smell good.”

“Are you drunk or something?” My body gradually grew hot and I pretended to walk to the window. “If you’re tired, you can rest first.”

He hugged me from behind and murmured: “I’m sober, Ant”

I turned to him and faced those eyes. They looked witchy as if they were some devil luring me to a trap. I, though acting tough, found myself enshrouded by the immanent passion over the years, hence the obliteration of my defense. I tried to push him away, but the tipsiness had already started to do its part. What lingered in my memory was his lips laden with fags slowly pressing against mine, so mild and sweet, that I could not help playing along with this star-crossed romance.

I remembered that it was on 15 March 2002, that person and I exchanged a passionate kiss. It was our first time practising such a marital love, so it was still awkward. It, nevertheless, was short and boring. He then left and told me that he would come back the next day. That said, he did not show up as promised, but hung out with another girl instead. I looked at him with great disappointment. If there were anyone to blame, I would blame myself for being so dumb when trusting those fair lies. Since then, I had become outrightly distrustful of boys, men in this life.

Sweets are sweet, but eternity does not exist in my dictionary. So was the kiss. Although I had been yearning for this intimacy and caresses from him, doing so was beyond rectitude. It made me a cheap prostitute, a toy letting others use. Worse yet, I became a homewrecker in his relationship, a person that people disdain and look down on. I was so evil.

I tried to reassure myself and told him: “We should stop this.”

“Did I hurt you?”

“No, it’s just wrong. Caba, listen to me. All of this was just an accident. It’s because we’re drunk and did some stupid shit.”

Then I ran to the loo and locked myself in there, hiding from the enormity I just caused, whilst he was calling my name outside. My tears fell, but I thought that his wife’s tears were far more moving than mine.
