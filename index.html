About

No view from nowhere.

Society Through a Human Prism is a personal publication by Hewwy Huynh — a space for examining the world through literature, philosophy, society, culture, and ordinary human experience.

There is no claim to speak for a discipline, a school of thought, or anyone beyond the person writing here.

Just one human perspective, looking outward.
