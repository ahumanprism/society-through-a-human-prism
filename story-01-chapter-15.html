14 February 2019

Back to Literature

“Ant.”

“What’s wrong?”

“I’ve got something for you.” He took out a ring-like box and said: “Today is Valentine’s Day, so…”

“Are you serious? We…”

“I know you’ve not had any title yet, but I’ve always chosen you from the beginning. It’s just I don’t know that the person I’ve been looking for is right next to me.”

I curtsied and said: “Thank you.”

I inertly woke up after a long sleep. I wondered how long I slept, but the sun reached its zenith outside. I lit a fag for myself and agonised over the dream. “If it were so beautiful, why would I still awake?” I murmured. After all this time, I found myself haunted by him. Naturally, I had signed some intangible contract, which even though I tried to break, I would undergo great suffering. “Are you well, Caba?”

After the imprudence, he and I had had a quite drawn-out separation. Even when we talked, it would be unrelieved quarrels. My inner self struggled with how to apologise to his wife as well as how to erase such a filthy trace. But he told me that it was his fault, and I should not feel guilty. I wondered what the difference was between his doing and mine? I meant that we both did things, and passing the buck had no meaning. Why not ending everything and parting ways?

Last August, when I was packing for Ireland, he himself came to say goodbye. That feeling evoked some weird nostalgia within me. It was like the day he joined the navy, but I was not there to see him the last time. But this time was different. Perhaps it would be a one-way departure, for if I remained here for any longer, it would weigh on my conscience to death. And he might not live well for the rest of his life.

Before leaving, I left a few presents he had given me. On further reflection, having them brought along would make no use, even probably making me come away with the past. He, on the other hand, was so determinedly said that they were but friend-like presents and were not worthy of being banished. Sensing his tone, I reluctantly carried them with me, but I was not sure if I would ever use them again.

Fortunately, I had stopped writing for a year, or else the media might put me on the spot. Our relationship was not considered noticeable in general, so I seemed not to gain notoriety for being a homewrecker. Intentionally or unintentionally, I had stolen him from his wife. It was so pitifully tragic. If I had chosen Henry, my life might have been better than now.

“Can you just stay?” he held my wrist, saying.

“What’s the point of staying then?”

“I…”

“You don’t even know the answer, so why keep advising me?”

“But I know that if you leave, I may not see you again.”

“I have my life. You have yours. Don’t you see the good?”

“Without you, I can’t carry on.”

“What do you mean by that?”

“Look, we’ve been together for more than ten long years. It’s been always you and only you by my side. It’s just I was so neglected, only caring about having a family and not knowing that you’ve been the one. It’s just my feelings. I thought that you’re just a friend. I was dumb. I should have realised sooner.”

“…”

“Do you remember, when you’re officiating, it may sound ridiculous, but looking at you, I felt like I did something horrible, so horrible that I hurt you. I saw you crying at that time, but you’re always good at hiding your emotions, not wanting me to be worried. But I saw through your tricks. And I decided not to let you be like that. My happy family is not a smokescreen of my love for you. I beg you please, just stay, not only because of me, but you yourself. You deserve good things and the happiness that you’ve always looked for.”

“But don’t you see it makes me a third party in your relationship? I did love you from the beginning, but that doesn’t mean I have the right to destroy your family. It’s because I love you, so I decide to resign myself to leaving. So please, Pedro, let me go. Our pain will be healed by time…”

“By time? Why are you so naive? You know, after that day, I’ve not been able to focus on everything, work or family. I’m like an empty shell. I underestimated your place in me. Losing you, I’m like a half-dead man.”

“…”

“Do you remember that I pissed you off when sneaking up on your novel? It was the first time that I found you cute and stubborn. And during that time when we’re not talking, I was in distress. I thought it’s because of the training, the new environment, but then I knew that it was because I underestimated my love for you.”

“But after all, you’re not bold enough to choose me. I’m just your second choice.”

“It’s because I was blind, so dumb that I didn’t realise that there’s always been a place for you in my heart. I thought it’s was a close friendship or some sort of soulmate. But when you’re touched by that fucking Sean, I just wanted to kill him. What I did was not only about revenge, but taking back your integrity, your honour. And…” he paused. “And showing him that you’re mine.”

“I’m yours? Since when?” I frowned, questioning.

“Since the first time I met you. Those small eyes, the mole under your right eye, or your coolness were what made me worried. At first, I thought you’re a strong, tough man, but when getting to know more about you, I thought that you’re childish and warm. But I didn’t think much. I simply thought that you’re green, so I decided to befriend you. And that association led us to the present.”

“…”

“I’m sorry, Ant. I’m really sorry. I know that I’m a dumb asshole. But please, trust me, my heart for you is real. I swear to God that what I’ve said to you so far contains no lie. My love for you is real. The feeling to be with you is real. So please, don’t you just leave?”

Whilst I was soaking in the bath, I unconsciously stroked some strand of my hair. I, in fact, had already donated it. Shortly after arriving Ireland, I signed up for hair donation at a hospital in my area. The moment I saw my dear hair gradually falling, I realised that hoping for something that was not us to stay was no different from a gull. It was me who made the decision from the beginning, so keeping it meant nothing.

What made me most emotional, however, was the girl wearing my hair, the jet-black hair with those ocean eyes of the girl stricken with acute cancer since she was born. She looked at me, happily smiling, and said: “Thank you so much. I’ve always wanted long hair like a princess and my wish has finally come true!” I hugged her, weeping, not only to show her my empathy, but to hug her dreams, her aspirations that would never be fulfilled.

My phone rang, and I quickly pulled a towel around me to pick it up. The person on the end of the line belonged to a woman, or more precisely, it was her voice, Isabel, her wife. There, I also heard the cry of a baby, which must have been his son, Martín. I could sense her preoccupation and frustration in her voice. She told me that I should go to Southampton General Hospital to take stock of his well-being. I was stunned, not even minding to ask how he was but hurriedly coming there. I was fidgety, unable to think clearly. All I could do was praying, hoping that he might not get much harmed.

Upon seeing him lying on the sickbed, covered in wounds, my heart stopped for a moment. Her wife said that it was because he was too drunk at some nightclub, and spoke coarsely and ended up inviting trouble. “Did he speak like that? I’ve known him for years, but…”

“But don’t you think it’s because of you?”

I frowned and replied: “What do you mean?”

“Usually, he may smoke and drink. But after your absence, he not only throws himself into those things, but he also comes to some shitty nightclubs and makes trouble. The boy and I can’t stand him any more.”

“I’m sorry…”

“I know what happened between the two of you. I don’t care about homosexuality, but you made him mad. You should look at yourself.”

“I just wanted the best for you two.”

“’The best you wanted was not really good. Truth will out. You should talk to him.”

“Talk? We have nothing to talk about.”

“I think you two have more to talk about than I do.”

“I’m sorry, Isabel. I thought my leaving was good for both sides. I didn’t think that this would happen.” I tried to catch what she said. Actually, I must thank him for inspiring me to study Spanish, so that I could listen to her lecturing on me. For shame!

“Compared to you, he and I have not been together for too long, but my love for him is sincere.” She sobbed, continuing: “Sadly, I was just his idle fun. We met. Our lives intersected, but I forgot that you’ve been with him, side by side.”

“…”

“I read some of his letters writing to you when he was in the navy and when you felt low. I’m sorry to your accident, but that didn’t mean you had to push him away. He chose you, so no matter what I did, his heart was never for me.”

“I’m really sorry.”

“It’s sad that if you two had talked me through the dilemma, everything might not have been too bad.”

She leaned on me and burst out crying, and so did I. What she said was not wholly absurd. What I meant good was not always good. Even though I did that behind her back, we should have dealt with the problem face-to-face, instead of turning her into a clown in this untitled relationship. It was a shame that life is unpredictable. I felt myself weak and spineless.
