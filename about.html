Society Through a Human Prism is an independent publication by Hewwy Huynh — an attempt to examine the world from one particular human perspective.

The Publication

This is a space for essays, fiction, reflections, and observations about the things that make up human experience.

Society, philosophy, psychology, literature, culture, and ordinary life often overlap in ways that are difficult to separate. This publication exists to explore those intersections.

There is no completely neutral place from which to look at the world. Every perspective belongs to someone.
