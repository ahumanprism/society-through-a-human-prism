About

No view
from nowhere.

Society Through a Human Prism is an independent publication by Hewwy Huynh — an attempt to look at the world through one particular human perspective.

The publication

This is a place for things I write.

Some of them take the form of essays. Some become stories, poems, or longer works. Others may simply begin as an idea that I want to examine more closely.

There is no particular discipline this publication belongs to, and no particular category every piece needs to fit into.

There is no completely neutral place from which to look at the world. Every perspective belongs to someone.
What lives here

Essays that question things I once took for granted.

Fiction about people, relationships, and whatever else a story happens to become.

Poems, fragments, observations, and ideas that may not need to belong anywhere else.

About the author

Hewwy Huynh is the writer behind Society Through a Human Prism.

There is not much more to explain.

This publication is simply one person's attempt to understand, question, imagine, and write about the world as they encounter it.
