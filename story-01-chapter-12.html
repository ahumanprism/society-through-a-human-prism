On my high school graduation day, I did not show up. I heard that I was in a coma and had been hospitalised for nearly a week, waiting for the diagnosis. When I was fully conscious, I remembered, in my dream, I made out my mother calling my name over and over again. “Nam, darling…” I kept running to her, but the more I ran, the more achy my legs felt. No matter how hard I attempted to crawl and slither, I could not catch up with her.

The sun outside had yet to awake. The curtains were fluttering in the breezes of late spring, cold and forlorn. I was lying on bed, eyes fixed on the ceiling, caressing the necklace Pedro had given me. In only a few more hours, I would become an undergraduate, a person entitled to holding an official degree. To be honest, I was not even nervous. There was one thing, though: I missed him. We had not met for months, and my heart was longing for him quite often. I heard that he had a lover, and she was Spanish, perhaps. He said that they both met in his hometown. Alive outside, dead inside—that was what I could describe my feelings when I heard the news.

“Twenty-seven already…” I murmured. Wandering far from home for years was truly gruelling. Thanks to him, however, my life could be able to welcome some youthful exultancy. He made me so special. I loved him and, in an excruciating way of speaking, I loved their relationship. I simply wished they could live in fulfilment, in eternity, and in harmony. As for me, the next chapters of my life might not be inscribed with his quotes. It was time, for my book, to come to an end.

During my internship, I happened to work with Henry. He kept roaming and planned to give me some sort of surprise. Although I tried to be out of his sight, I occasionally unavoidably had to go with him in meetings and parties with the company. He even threatened to say things about my gender identity to Pedro if I refused to give him one more chance of asking me out. I, at that moment, looked at him contemptuously and said: “It’s not your turn to do so.” Henry, as I recalled, looked pitifully vindictive and told this story to the boss shortly after that, but I was not worried at all. If I, at worst, was fired, I could find other places to intern. Fortunately, the boss took this for granted and swept aside all of his accusations of me. It seemed that I plucked out the thorn in my flesh with success.

Around six thirty, I was preparing myself prior to being present at the hall. My hair was already much longer than before, so it was quite time-consuming to get everything settled. My long hair was partly for style, but most importantly, I wished to donate it to whoever needed it. When Pedro found out the plan, he, without hesitation, embraced me and told me that I had a good-hearted soul. He also brushed his lips against my hair and said: “Honestly, the hair suits you. It smells good too.”

I looked at my reflection in the mirror and wondered if the hair was what I wanted or merely what pleased him. I faintly smiled and fixed the mortarboard. If the erstwhile promise was still in effect, I was fully eligible to be his officiant. It was only that would I be able to hold back my tears in his grand day? Or should I act as if my best friend finally had his own family, and then I had the right to cry like that?

Last month, Pedro and I went to a bar with his fellows. The common impression of me was that I looked like a girl. He, at the same time, forced a smile and told everyone that he would pick up the tab and they should feel free to order drinks. He also put his arm around my shoulders to imply that I was his best friend, and they should pay respect to me. The music was pounding, but my heart was subtly racing.

After a while, I went to the loo to relieve myself. Since my hair was being tucked into a cap, drawing attention was undoable. I washed my face and looked at my reflection in the mirror facing the urinal. As soon as I was about to light a fag, one of his fellows came and did it for me. “Thanks,” I said.

“So you’re the writer whom Pedro often talks about?”

“You’re flattering me. And you’re…”

“I’m Sean. You’re Anthony, right?”

I nodded. There was indeed a Sean amongst his fellows. Pedro once told me about him, that he was friendly and rather quiet, but not that sullen. That said, he was like Henry, loving to breed freely. This hangout, I supposed, must be his stop, and now he was searching for some holes to fill in.

After saying a few things, I said goodbye and walked away. But he, in the absence of the others, pulled me in the urinal, forcefully and determinedly, and had something on his mind, something dirty and immoral. On instinct, I fought back, but his strength, like Pedro’s, completely dominated me. He covered my mouth and tried to pull out that disgusting, lustful thing, so that he could find himself pleased. On the other hand, I figured out that he was not gay or bisexual, but simply wanted to taste what homosexual intercourse felt like. I told him that I had no interest in him, but he believed that I was pretending and, in reality, I was craving for men than ever. What he said truly sickened me, and my self-esteem was therefore violated until wholly debased.

At my lowest, Pedro turned up. He saw me not coming back, so he decided to check on me. He said that he had some hunch that warned him about my safety, and indeed, he was right. He kicked the door, storming in and beating him up. I tried to stop him and pull him away from that hell, but he said that he had to stand up for me and then kicked him in the testicles to teach him to control his beastly lust. The situation was too chaotic that when I looked back, I felt myself living in it, unable to escape from that execrable nightmare.

I took a pair of scissors in the drawer to purposely cut this hair. I always blamed myself that since I grew the hair, the jinx in me had seemed to grow more obvious. Only little things could make my day collapse and my mood sink into the abyss. Then I crumpled to the ground and burst out crying. Even I had no idea why I cried. I just knew that I sat there, in the room, and sobbed for a while before drifting off on the floor.

About two weeks ago, he invited me to hang around at his house. It looked so different now, not because of its new ornamentation, but because it was about to become the home of him and his woman only. I was sitting at the table with a languid face whilst he was making lunch for both. Despite the spring time, I had struggled to cover myself in another layer of a hoodie. After escaping from the maelstrom, I always had a feeling that I was being touched by someone, so I chose to guard myself in those chunky hoodies. He also gave me one of his to remind me that I was always protected, even when he was not by my side, some sort imagery, one might say.

We did not talk throughout the lunch. After taking some food affectedly, I went upstairs, as I was not feeling quite well. He was helpless and let me be. These days carried a lugubrious sense. There was a divide between us that held us back, making conversations unthinkable. Whenever I uttered a few words, I tried to avoid him and glossed over other subjects. I could sense his helplessness. But please, dear Pedro, accept my apology. There was nothing that I could do. The mental wound was grievous. However I tried to purify myself in different ways, I would never ever be clean again. I became rubbishy now.

“Ant, how do you feel?” He came to the bed but did not touch me. He must have been aware of how touches could be catastrophic to me. “Do you feel like going to the hospital?”

“I’m fine. Don’t worry about me.”

“Why?” he said in a sympathetic voice. “Seeing you like this, I feel so helpless and sad.”

“Caba, don’t be… You tried to save my face. That was very kind of you. No one could ever expect such an accident…” I sobbed. “I’m okay.”

“Can I hug you… for a while?” he faltered.

I bobbed my head repeatedly. It was this very bed where joy and sorrow were bespoken. But very soon, I might not be able to lie on this cosy bed. His warmth made its way to every dark nook of me; yet, it seemed not to care about that harmless fire. To put it bluntly, it was me who was ashamed of facing him. I felt unclean in his eyes and indecently stained to love him anymore. My last purity was stolen by that monster. What it left was trauma and marks on the body that, even when I died, would never be reversed.

Stirring awake, I saw Pedro next to me, his hands holding mine very dearly. Back then, I was preparing for the graduation, but now I was lying sprawled here. “What a pity,” I mumbled. Then he hugged me and said: “There you are. You scared me, idiot.”

“Caba…”

He dissected my body and gently said: “Are you hurt?”

“I’m fine.”

“‘Fine, fine’. You say that all the time. Look how fragile you are now!”

I smiled, replying: “Do I look too awful?”

“You do, like a fucking corpse.”

“Corpse? Sounds literally awful. What time is it now?”

“Eleven.”

“Have I been asleep for that long?”

“You slept like a log.”

“Well, I guess I couldn’t give the speech then.”

“Who told you that? You can give me the speech, my top writer.”

I blushed to look at him and said: “But I’m not sure if you’re interested in boring advice.”

“As long as it’s from your heart, advice will never be boring.”

I beamed at him, asking: “How do I look in this gown?”

“Very handsome.”

“You’re handsome too, Caba.”

He patted me on the head and said: “Then let me hug you to prove your honesty.”

“You’re asking too much.”

I let him hug me as long as he wanted. Without saying, he must have been into a state. I suddenly felt guilty for dragging him in the mess of me. He, at last, was here, so I found myself somewhat relieved. He truly treated so well that I wished to be his slave in another life, if any, so that he could raise his chin and hold his head high.
