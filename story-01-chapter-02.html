One life, one love, forever

Chapter Two

12 June 2007

THE FIRST STORY

These summer days, it seemed to get hotter and hotter, but compared with my hometown, the hot climate here in Southampton was bearable. I was sitting in the cabin drawing to refresh the mind. My artistic sensibility remarkably changed since primary school. I meant it was not as extraordinarily sublime as that of world-famous figures, such as Leonardo da Vinci, Vincent van Gogh, or Claude Monet. Basically, it was good-looking, not too awful nor too marvellous, to drive people madly intrigued.

Pedro came in and saw me mesmerised with my own affair. He said nothing, simply sitting opposite to me and lighting the fag. Recently, he appeared to keep conversing with me, which could have been considered a lengthy annal. I knew that he had no company that were around his age, but it was unfortunate for him that I had no interest in making male friends or, more precisely, male peers. Perhaps, it was because I was downcast, antisocial, and tedious, so I did not desire to communicate with human beings.

He was the inverse. He kept talking, though he was not quite a talkative person, but most conversations between us, he outtalked me. Besides, I noticed that his face invariably glistened with youthful joy. I did not know, but that happy face sometimes prompted me to feel jealous. I wondered how people could wear such a jubilant mask during a very long time. I could barely do so.

And as previous times, he probably did not stand this overwhelming atmosphere like military enlistment, so his mouth finally moved.

“Your drawing is cool.”

“Thank you.”

After that, he suddenly lowered his voice and asked: “You hate me?”

I looked at him in amazement and said: “No. What’s wrong?”

“Nothing,” he replied and soon turned away. “Sometimes, I feel that you’re trying to avoid me.”

“I am not.” He, by chance, touched a raw nerve, which made me uneasy.

“Then hang out with me after work. Us, cigarettes, beers, shit talk, that sort of thing.”

“Sí, sí, señor.” Reluctantly, I accepted his offer, whilst he grinned. I supposed that there was nothing fun to laugh at.

It was seven right now. Streetlights showed up by turns as luminescent mushrooms in the twilight. I combed my hair and adjusted my outfit for the walk with Pedro. Looking at my reflection in the mirror, I saw myself changed in an unrecognisable way. It was indisputable that change is constant, but I seemed to become another version of that man. I hate him, but I could not negate the fact that flesh and blood within my body were moulded by him. Even the face, the jawline, or the facial features of me were granted by him. Apparently, I did not dare to repudiate such gifts.

Because of the slight coolness, I simply wore a pair of shorts and a black shirt embroidered with alternate white seam lines. Despite being a man, I hardly found a strand of body hair. In other words, except for that part, the proportion of hair of my body was sparse, even when it came to the beard.

But my physique stood out most. I got bigger and more muscular since high school. It must have been hard work and strict diet that made the change inevitably normal. Yet, I felt sorry for myself and other people, for we were under judgement of society. At the bottom of my heart, I would love to be a little boy so as to be shielded by my husband. Would he love me and this body? Or was it because of my oversensitivity with everything?

I arrived at the park nearby as promised and noticed that Pedro was waiting for me. I wondered how long he had been sitting there. As soon as seeing me, he started his catchphrase.

“Hola amigo!”

I looked at him and said nothing. His outfit was seemingly intended for a runaway fashion show. It was all simple, like a pair of navy skinny leg jeans, a plain white shirt, and a black coat. In fact, his face was the centre of attention, which could have convinced the others that he was attending some couture presentations. He was of typical Spanish countenance that I had always perceived, with a sharp nose, thick eyebrows and eyelashes, brown eyes and, in particular, a rather chic moustache.

His body was what I got fed up with. When we were in the sea, he was often bare-chested, which revealed his hefty physique. Compared to his, mine was nothing. I was not quite sure if it was the epitome of masculinity, but I believed that it was his discipline and effort that turned him into a massive person, through which I truthfully admired and was impressed to a certain extent.

We walked along the beach, each of us having our own beer and fag. The air here was much better than we were on the ship. The temperature and the stars altogether embellished the surrounding landscape, which somewhat ameliorated my languid mood. I plopped down on the white sand and gazed at those blinking pearls in the sky, attempting to seize the moment.

Pedro sat right next to me. He stared at the dark horizon from a distance and contemplated. Although we were bodily present, our soul drifted elsewhere. One minded their own business without minding the other’s.

A shooting star from nowhere flashed through the sky. I quietly prayed for my peaceful nation, my happy family, and self-peace.

“Self-peace? I wonder if I could have it in my lifetime…”

Unexpectedly, Pedro turned to me, and he pressed his face right up against mine. Our faces were so close that our lips could have collided.

Startled, I shouted: “What the fuck!”

“I’m helping you to go back to reality.” He grinned.

I calmly regulated my breathing. Earlier, that was so inconceivable to me. Normally, he looked quite serious, but sometimes acted weird, comporting himself with unprecedented silliness.

Then I stubbed out the fag in the sand and reached out a beer prepared beforehand. Taking a sip, I tried to regain my composure. He, nevertheless, was reasonable. I maybe ought to live in the real world. Fantasy might contain no suffering, but mere monotony. Reality, by contrast, might have earthly aesthetics, but lacked placidity.

I was grateful for what he did. He, in the meantime, apologised for unsettling me. Reading between the lines, I found that he was not that shallow compared to those I had met before. At times, his manner stirred my yearnings, which I extinguished all in the end, or else I would have been the person tormented by my absurd thought.

He turned to me, frowning, and said with his wistful eyes: “You got any plan for the future? Or just keep working here?”

“I’m going to study at a university this September.” At first, I planned to tell the captain about my next journey, but it seemed man proposed, but God disposed.

“Really?” he stuttered. “You… I’m a bit surprised.”

“I’m quite reserved, so no one knows about this until now, except you.”

“You’re fucking cool.”

“Thanks.”

“What are you studying?”

“English Literature.”

“You love Literature?”

I nodded. Love was not enough, but worship. It was what I often leant on whenever I faced hardships in life, and it was what allowed me to bury myself in fictitious, deceptive stories. Albeit unreal, they at least made me less empty. Unless the world was void of language, I would carry on leaving literary works for posterity.

To sate my curiosity, I took advantage of the situation and enquired:

“And you? What’s your plan?”

He shrugged. “Maybe, I’d keep working here, save up some money, and settle down.”

“How simple.” His dream was apparently what many people desired. Having a stable life, a stable job, and a stable home sounded simple. Still, not everyone could achieve it and had the ability to obtain such a dream. I personally wanted to build my own home. Deep down in my heart, there had always been a wife waiting for his husband to return home. It had been a long time since I thought of it.

“But it’d be hard, you know.” He took a puff and continued: “I mean I’m young and dumb, and I’m afraid I can’t take care of people I love. That’s why I don’t think much of it. I just take it as motivation to keep going.”

He concealed his disappointment behind a faint smile, but my sensibility did not let him pull the wool over my eyes.

“You have dreams and free will. I believe you can get what you deserve.” I reassured him with soothing words that I had been longing for being listened. “I think your dream may be simple, but it’s not insignificant at all. You said you’re not mature enough, but you have plenty of time ahead, and we’ve got a lot to learn. It’s just that if you feel under pressure, have a rest instead. Don’t be so harsh on yourself. Pride comes before a fall, you know.”

“Thank you.” He let out a beam that I had not ever seen since I met him. Unconsciously, I felt I was the one making him proud, and that made me proud of myself too.

“Good luck with the studies.”

“Gracias, señor.”

“I mean it. I really admire your diligence. I dropped out when I finished secondary school, so I’m impressed by studious people like you.”

“Did something happen?”

“Back then, my family was poor, and the high school fee was clearly too much for us, so I told my mother that I wanted to pack it in to help her make ends meet. I also have a younger sister. I thought leaving school was for her sake.”

His eyes fell on the distant raging waves.

“I’m so sorry to hear that.”

“It’s alright. It’s long gone. I feel very happy now.”

Literally, I was rather astounded. The first thing that amazed me was his mannerism. If he had not mentioned his early school leave, I would have never been aware of his academic background, as I had supposed he owned a bachelor’s degree or something. I mean he did look daft sometimes, but he proved himself an educated man, not some street thug he confessed. Until then, he had been a kind person to me.

“You’ll definitely be a man that everyone can rely on. I believe so,” I said.

We simply sat there and rambled until late evening, and we parted ways after that. He even suggested escorting me home by his motorbike, but I bluntly turned him down and said that if he kept following me, I would fall out with him.

The moon was bright like the other days. I looked out of the window to marvel at the stars reigning in the darkness. That being said, unlike the other days, today I smiled a lot.
